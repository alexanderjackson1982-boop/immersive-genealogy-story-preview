This website is public; your GEDCOM stays in this browser. GitHub records ordinary website-visit information, including IP addresses, for security. The preview does not upload your file, stories or feedback. Only use it on a device and browser you trust.

The existing viewer’s privacy filter hides living, unknown, recently deceased, private and contradictory records. Only eligible people with recorded deaths before 1926 are shown. Notes, contact fields, media, occupations and source prose are omitted.

Tree, stories and feedback are held only for this browser session. Clear, refresh or leaving the page removes them from the preview. Access expires after 30 minutes without activity or two hours in total.

Your original file is unchanged. Automatic filtering cannot guarantee anonymity in names and places; check before sharing screenshots or downloaded feedback.
